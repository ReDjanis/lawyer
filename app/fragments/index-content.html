About us

The Andreeva Legal Consultancy LLC law consulting firm was founded by Lina Andreeva, a professorial lawyer who has been living in Dubai since 1994. After completing her studies in London and receiving a Master's degree in Law from the London Graduate School of Economics, Lina Andreeva began practicing law at one of the leading law firms in Dubai in 2007.

The beginning of her career coincided with the adoption of Law No. 7 of 2006, which gave foreign citizens the right to purchase real estate in the Emirate of Dubai (UAE) (then other emirates followed the example of Dubai) . With the adoption of this key law, a completely new direction in legal consulting in the UAE has emerged, namely legal consulting in the field of real estate transactions, since before that real estate transactions were carried out only between citizens of the UAE and Gulf Cooperation Countries (GCC).
