About us

Andreeva Legal Consultancy LLC is a legal consulting firm founded by professional lawyer Lina Andreeva, who has been living in Dubai since 1994. After completing her studies in London and obtaining a Master of Laws degree from the London School of Economics, Lina Andreeva began her legal practice in one of Dubai’s leading law firms in 2007.

The start of her career coincided with the enactment of Law No. 7 of 2006, which granted foreign nationals the right to purchase real estate in the Emirate of Dubai (UAE), a move that was later followed by other emirates. The adoption of this landmark law led to the emergence of an entirely new field of legal consultancy in the UAE — real estate transactions advisory — as prior to this, property transactions were limited to citizens of the UAE and the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) countries.

  • Real Estate Law

    Andreeva Legal Consultancy represents buyers, sellers, developers, real estate brokers (both legal entities and individuals) and contractors in all legal aspects of Dubai’s real estate sector. We regularly draft and negotiate real estate agreements and manage complex transactions with precision. Our real estate practice includes:

    • Sale and purchase of residential and commercial properties
    • Tenancy contracts
    • Long-term lease and management agreements
    • Construction contracting
    • Real estate financing and mortgaging
    • Representation of interests by Power of Attorney
    • Remote issuance of all types of notarized powers of attorney (without physically visiting a notary, and even if the client is located outside the UAE)

    One of the services in this area is representing one of the parties (the buyer or the seller) in a real estate transaction by Power of Attorney. Often, clients grant a power of attorney to a real estate broker, not realizing that this creates a conflict of interest: the broker is directly financially interested in closing the deal and receiving a commission, rather than in protecting the client’s legal interests. In such cases, the client also loses control over the transaction, creating opportunities for financial manipulation. To avoid such situations, it is better to involve a licensed lawyer to oversee the purchase or sale of real estate.
